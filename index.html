English

Car Salvage Simulator is a single-player game about running a car salvage business. It has no user accounts, chat or user uploads. This policy explains what data the game and the services built into it handle.

Data stored only on your device

  • Game progress (workshop, vehicles, parts, money, missions, collection) in local save files, including backup copies.
  • Settings such as language, sound, controls, vibration, graphics quality and notification preference.
  • Local diagnostics: gameplay events and error records kept in small files on the device (capped in size) to help the game run correctly. The game does not send these files to us.

If you use the save export feature, the exported code or file contains your game progress only. It is created and shared only when you choose to.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded ads and may show other ads, provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the game settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. The game receives purchase confirmations from Google Play to unlock what you bought and to restore purchases.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when a tool order arrives). These are created on the device; no notification data is sent to us.

What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers that collect your gameplay data.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos inside the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you reset the game, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app; doing so can permanently delete your progress. Data processed by Google AdMob and Google Play is kept under Google's policies. Because we hold no account or server-side game record for you, there is nothing for us to delete on a server; for questions about data handled by the built-in services, contact us and we will help.

Contact and changes

Questions or requests: mehmeta.balbayy+carsalvage@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords or payment details. If the game's data handling changes, we will update this page and the Google Play data safety section before releasing the change; the effective date above shows the current version.
