Privacy policy

Single-player game that works offline for normal play. There is no game chat. Online features need a connection: Google Firebase analytics and crash reports, Google ads, Google Play purchases and the optional cloud save, and two small services we run on Google Apps Script (purchase, reward and security verification, and the feedback form). Progress and preferences stay on your device; if you link a Google account (Google Play Games), the save is also stored in your Google cloud so you can continue on another device. Game money has no cash value.

Developer and data controller: Mehmet Agah Balbay, an individual developer based in Türkiye, publishing the game on Google Play. Privacy contact: the email address shown at the top of this page.

Local storage

Saves hold vehicles, inventory, workshop, money, missions, purchase entitlements and reward limits. Preferences include language, audio, controls and graphics. A random install ID (not your name, email or Google account) is created on first launch and kept on the device; it is reset when you clear app data or reinstall. Local crash diagnostics contain only UTC timestamps and error categories, never raw messages or stack traces, and are kept on your device in up to five files of 256 KiB each. A copy of gameplay analytics is also kept on your device (analytics.jsonl, up to 512 KiB). The usage-data setting controls new local analytics, Firebase analytics and Firebase crash reports; it does not control advertising consent. Exported save codes contain progress: share them only with people you trust.

Analytics and crash reports — Google Firebase

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to learn how the game is used and to fix crashes. While the usage-data setting is on (the default), Firebase receives game events such as the day you reach, shop level, tutorial steps, unlocks, rewarded-ad and purchase screens viewed, session length and language, and technical play signals such as frame rate, loading time, repeated taps on the same spot, a screen left open for a long time and the last screens before the game was closed, together with an app instance identifier, device model, Android version, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address, and, after a crash, crash and diagnostic data such as the stack trace and device state. Game events do not contain your name, email address, save contents or payment details. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Firebase stores analytics data only for the purposes you accept in the Google consent form shown at first launch (Google Consent Mode); until then, and if you decline, analytics storage stays off. Firebase Remote Config also downloads game settings, which uses the same app instance identifier. Google processes this data as our service provider under the Firebase terms and its privacy policy. Turn off the usage-data setting in Settings to stop sending new analytics and crash reports; data already sent stays with Google under its retention rules (Firebase Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months). Request deletion through the contact address below, quoting the app instance identifier if you know it.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may process IP address (approximate location), advertising/device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Data handling depends on consent, region and SDK settings. Rewarded ads use server-side verification: Google AdMob sends our verification service your random install ID and a one-time code so that a reward is confirmed once (see the verification section below). The Supporter Pack disables interstitials and grants rewarded-ad rewards without watching, under the same daily limits. Rewarded ads remain optional; declining an ad grants no ad reward.

Privacy choices — Google UMP

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests current consent information and shows a privacy form when required. Ads are requested only when UMP permits. Settings offers ad privacy choices when required by UMP. You can also manage the advertising ID in Android settings. Buying Remove ads does not disable the SDK or replace privacy choices.

Purchases — Google Play Billing

Optional real-money purchases use Unity IAP with Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and the Play account. The game receives purchase identifiers, receipts and ownership information to validate, deliver and restore products. We do not receive your full payment card number. Transaction identifiers and entitlements are retained locally to prevent duplicate grants, and the purchase token and order ID are also sent to our verification service (see below).

Purchase, reward and security verification — our service

To prevent fraud and duplicate grants, the game sends data to a small verification service that we run on Google Apps Script (Google infrastructure). When you buy or restore a product, it receives the product ID, the Google Play purchase token and order ID, the app version and your random install ID. It checks the purchase with the Google Play Developer API and uses Google's list of refunded or voided orders to remove items that were refunded. When you watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob calls the same service with your random install ID and a one-time code (server-side verification).

Once per session while online, the game also asks Google Play Integrity for a signed token that says whether the app is genuine and installed from Google Play and whether the device passes Google's checks. The token goes only to our service, which has Google decode it and stores only a short result, the install ID and the app version. The game also runs checks on the device (genuine package and signature, root, emulator, code-injection tools, clock tampering) and may compare the phone clock with Google's time by sending a tiny request to google.com or gstatic.com, which receive your IP address like any website. These checks do not read your files, contacts, messages or location.

The install ID is random, is not linked to your name, email or Google account and changes if you clear app data or reinstall. The service's log (time, event type, product, order ID, result, install ID, app version) is kept in a private Google Sheet that only the developer can open, is not shared with anyone else, is not used for advertising and is deleted after 12 months.

Feedback form

The feedback form sends the text you write, the topic you choose and the optional email address you enter, together with the game version, device model, Android version, language, game day, shop tier and two random identifiers that are not linked to your save, to our support mailbox and to a private Google Sheet (both on Google Apps Script and Google Workspace infrastructure). If you are offline, the entry waits on your device and is sent when a connection returns. Leave the email field empty if you do not want a reply. We use feedback only to fix problems and improve the game, and keep it for up to 24 months or until you ask us to delete it.

Cloud save — Google Play Games Services

Optional. If you link your Google account in Settings > Account, the game signs in with Google Play Games Services and stores your save (vehicles, inventory, workshop, money, missions, purchase entitlements and reward limits, the same content as an exported save code) as a Saved Games snapshot in your Google account. It is written at most once every five minutes and when the app goes to the background, and read when the game starts. Google processes your Google account name, Play Games player ID and the snapshot under its own policy; the developer runs no server and receives no copy. If the cloud save is further along than the save on this device, the game asks before loading it and never replaces your progress silently. You can delete the saved game from your Google Play Games settings or Google account. Without a linked account nothing is uploaded.

Your rights, deletion and consent

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA and the UK under the GDPR, and Türkiye under Law No. 6698, KVKK), you can ask whether we process data about you, ask for a copy, correction or deletion, object to or restrict processing, ask for portability, withdraw consent at any time and complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority). We process data to run and secure the game (purchases, rewards, support) based on our contract with you and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and fixing bugs, and on your consent where the law requires it, for example for ads (Google UMP).

To make a request, email the contact address above. The game has no account, so we can find data only through identifiers you give us: the Google Play order number from your receipt (purchase and reward logs), the email address you typed in the feedback form (feedback), or the install ID or app instance ID if you know it. We reply within 30 days. Data held by Google (Firebase, AdMob, Play Games, Google Play) is deleted through Google; we pass your request on where we can.

You can also decide in the game: Settings > usage data switch stops new Firebase analytics and crash reports; the ad privacy choices in Settings (shown when required) reopen the Google consent form to change or withdraw ad consent; Android settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID; clearing app data or uninstalling deletes local data. Without a linked Google account nothing is uploaded to the cloud, and a saved game can be deleted in Google Play Games settings.

Retention and contact

Local data remains until reset, overwritten or removed through Android app storage settings or uninstall. New game does not erase all preferences, logs or backups. Android cloud backup is disabled in release builds; use the save export/import feature or the optional Google Play Games cloud save to transfer progress. Device-to-device transfer behavior can depend on Android and the device manufacturer. Google retains service data, including Firebase analytics and crash data, under its own policy. Email support correspondence is used to answer your request and retained only as needed for support or legal obligations; request deletion using the contact address above.

Retention periods: purchase, reward and security logs 12 months; feedback and support email up to 24 months or until you ask for deletion; Firebase Analytics event data up to 14 months; local data until you remove it. We do not sell your data.

International transfers: Google processes data in the United States and other countries where it operates, and our verification and feedback services run on Google infrastructure; the developer is based in Türkiye. Transfers rely on the safeguards Google provides, such as standard contractual clauses.

External services and children

AdMob, UMP, Firebase, Google Play, Google Play Games Services and the browser/website host process connection and service data under their own policies. The game has no social messaging. It is intended for people aged 13 and over (or the minimum age in your country) and does not ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it. Audience and content rating are shown in Google Play. We update this policy when processing changes.
